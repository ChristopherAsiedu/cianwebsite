A living legacy

Built on the vision
of Bishop Bill Hamon.

Christian International was founded by Dr. Bill Hamon, a prophet with more than six decades of ministry. His mission: to nurture and establish apostles and prophets, and to equip spiritual leaders to walk fully in their gifts and callings.

Today the Christian International Apostolic Network brings more than 900 US ministers together as one family, honoring that legacy and running with it.
